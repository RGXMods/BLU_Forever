# BLU Forever 1.1.1

Released 2026-09-20.

- The Death tracker is replaced by the Hardcore tab: four separate death
  sound triggers - your death (PLAYER_DEAD), party member death, raid
  member death, and other player death (combat-log driven, player deaths
  only, gated by the framework's combatLogEvent capability). All four
  default to None and each has its own volume and channel setting.
- The tab grid now matches Retail and Classic exactly, with the Hardcore
  tab taking the Retail "Future 1" slot and Future 2-6 restored as
  greyed-out placeholders.
- Greyed-out flavor tabs are no longer clickable.
